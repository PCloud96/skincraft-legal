Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

Introduction

Cloud Motion Lab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Skin Craft mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. Short version: we don't collect personal data, we don't have accounts, we don't track you across apps, and we don't sell anything to anyone.

Information We Collect

Anonymous Usage Analytics: We use TelemetryDeck to collect fully anonymous usage signals — which skins get viewed, which get saved, how long sessions last. This data is aggregated and does not identify you. TelemetryDeck does not use cookies, IDFAs, or any persistent identifier tied to you.

No Personal Data: Skin Craft does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal information. There are no user accounts.

No Purchases: Skin Craft is completely free. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no payment data of any kind.

Photos Library Access

When you tap "Save to Photos" on a skin, Skin Craft requests permission to add that image to your device's Photos library. We only ADD images. We do not read, scan, or access any existing photos in your library. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Data Storage

Skin Craft stores nothing on remote servers. All app data (your onboarding state, settings, whether you've seen the review prompt) is stored locally on your device using Apple's UserDefaults. Deleting the app removes all this data.

Third-Party Services

  • TelemetryDeck: Anonymous analytics. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Handles app distribution and ratings. Privacy Policy

Children's Privacy

Skin Craft is rated 9+ and is safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to delete or export. Uninstalling the app removes all local data instantly.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
